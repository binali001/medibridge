The problem

Every referral starts from zero.

A patient sees a doctor in one clinic, then turns up at another - a new city, an emergency room, a locum on a night shift. Their history doesn't travel with them: allergies, prior diagnoses, medications, test results. The clinician is left guessing, and the patient is left repeating themselves at the worst possible moment.

Paper folders get lost. Systems don't talk to each other. And in an emergency, the seconds spent asking "do you have any allergies?" to someone who can't answer are seconds that matter.

i.

Fragmented records. Each hospital or clinic keeps its own file - often on paper, rarely shared with the next provider.

ii.

Emergencies without context. Unconscious or unaccompanied patients arrive with no way to communicate allergies, conditions, or medications.

iii.

Repeated, costly tests. Without access to prior results, providers re-run diagnostics that already exist somewhere else on paper.

The solution

One Health ID. One card. Every provider.

MediBridge issues every patient a unique Health ID, tied to a QR-enabled Health Card. Scan it, and an authorized provider sees exactly what's relevant - history, allergies, ongoing treatments - with the patient's consent built into every access.

No app required to benefit from it. No new hospital software to install. Just a card that carries the truth of a patient's care from one door to the next.

How it works

From registration to bedside, in three steps.

1

Get a Health ID

A patient registers once - at a partner clinic, hospital, or community outreach point - and receives their MediBridge Health ID and Health Card.

2

A provider scans

At any participating facility, staff scan the QR code. The patient confirms access, just as they would hand over a paper file.

3

Care, informed

Relevant history appears immediately - allergies, medications, past diagnoses - so treatment starts from knowledge, not guesswork.

Who it's for

Built around the patient, useful to everyone treating them.

Patients

Carry your own history with you, and decide who gets to see it - across every clinic, hospital, or emergency room you visit.

Providers

Treat with context from the first minute. Fewer repeated tests, fewer blind guesses, safer decisions.

Emergency responders

When a patient can't speak for themselves, their Health Card can - allergies and conditions, available in seconds.
